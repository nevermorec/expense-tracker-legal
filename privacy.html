Expense Tracker — Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 5 月 5 日 · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

轻迹是一款本地优先的记账 App。手动记账数据存储在设备及你启用的 iCloud 私人数据库中。AI 记账为可选功能：选中的完整图片或提交的文字会经我们的服务器发送至火山引擎方舟（豆包模型）。这些内容可能包含个人信息，请在发送前裁剪或删除不需要的信息。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于「轻迹记账」（包名 com.expense.ExpenseTracker）。以下明确同意、拒绝及撤回机制适用于包含「AI 数据与隐私」页面的客户端（包括首发版本 1.0.0）；未包含该机制的旧测试客户端需更新后才可继续使用 AI。2026 年 11 月 17 日部署的后端已拦截未携带有效同意版本的识别请求。

2. AI 数据共享：发送什么、发送给谁

仅在你主动使用图片、文字、语音转写、分享扩展或快捷指令等 AI 识别功能，且已明确同意 AI 数据共享后，才发送识别内容。接收方为火山引擎方舟（豆包模型；服务提供方：北京火山引擎科技有限公司），用于提取金额、商户、日期、分类和付款方式等记账字段。

  • 图片：你当次选择或分享的完整图片、截屏。图片里的姓名、电话、地址、账号、订单及消费记录等个人信息也可能随图片发送；不会自动遮挡或脱敏。
  • 文字：你提交的完整文字，包括语音转写文字。其内包含的个人信息也会被发送。
  • 范围：不会自动扫描整个相册或将已有账本整体上传。请只选择你有权处理的内容，并在发送前裁剪、遮挡或删除无关信息。

传输路径为 App → 我们的 HTTPS 识别服务 → 火山引擎方舟北京区域 API（ark.cn-beijing.volces.com）。我们的后端把所选图片或文字作为识别输入发送给方舟；不会将额度标识或 Apple 签名交易凭证作为 AI 提示内容转发。

第三方对收到内容的处理和保留受其服务条款、适用配置及隐私政策约束。我们的请求设置不保存模型响应（store=false），但这不等于第三方系统完全不保留任何安全或运营记录。我们不作未经核实的第三方「零保留」或「不用于训练」承诺。请阅读 火山引擎隐私政策。

3. 你的选择与同意

  • 首次使用 AI 前，会展示接收方、用途、数据类别、保存说明及隐私链接；只有点击「同意并使用 AI」后才允许上传。开启旧版 AI 功能开关、相册选择或麦克风授权均不替代此同意。
  • 可选择「不同意，使用手动记账」，或关闭告知页。拒绝后不会发送新的识别内容，手动记账继续可用。
  • 可在「设置 → AI 数据与隐私」查看说明或撤回同意。撤回阻止未来的新识别上传，无法自动收回已经传输的内容。
  • 同意版本及时间保存在当前设备的 App 共享容器中，供主 App、分享扩展和快捷指令检查；不会作为账本数据同步到其他设备。未同意时，快捷指令会提示先在主 App 查看说明并同意。
  • 撤回 AI 同意不会自动取消 Apple 订阅；如不再需要会员，请另行取消订阅。

4. 其他数据与 Apple 服务

  • 手动账单：标题、金额、分类、日期、备注等保存在本机 SwiftData 数据库中。启用 iCloud 后通过 Apple CloudKit 同步到你的私人数据库，我们的识别服务器不会因此自动接收整本账单。你主动选择的识别输入仍按第 2 节处理。
  • 额度管理：App 向我们的后端发送随机设备标识，或由已验证交易派生的假名化额度主体标识，用于累计每日调用次数。这些标识不是不可关联的匿名数据。
  • 订阅验证：Apple StoreKit 处理购买和付款。App 可将 Apple 签名交易凭证发送至我们的后端验证订阅、产品及额度；凭证可能包含交易标识、商品、环境、有效期等信息。我们不会收到支付银行卡资料，也不将凭证发给 AI 模型。
  • 语音：使用 Apple Speech 转写，支持时优先要求设备端识别；不支持时可能由 Apple 在线处理。原始录音不会上传给我们的后端或方舟 AI；转写文字在你同意后按第 2 节发送。
  • 主动反馈：你通过意见反馈主动提交的文字用于处理支持问题，可能包含你自行填写的联系方式或其他个人信息。请避免提交无关敏感信息。
  • 诊断：你允许与开发者共享 Apple 分析数据时，Apple 可能向我们提供崩溃诊断报告。本 App 不使用广告 IDFA，不主动索取通讯录或定位权限。

Apple 服务适用 Apple 隐私政策。

6. 保存、安全与保留期

  • 识别内容：我们的识别服务在处理请求期间使用图片、文字和识别结果，不将这些内容写入业务日志或内容数据库。图片上传的临时表单文件在请求结束时清理；不建立识别内容档案。App 上的识别结果可由你确认后保存为本地账单。
  • 运行日志：保留时间、接口类别、状态、耗时等必要运行信息，不记录识别原文、模型原始输出、交易凭证或设备标识。应用日志按 UTC 日轮转，保留当日及此前 29 个日历日，定时清理。
  • 额度记录：数据库保存假名化主体、日期及每日次数等必要额度字段，保留当日及此前 29 个日历日；启动后和每小时执行过期清理。
  • 反馈：主动反馈另存于限制访问的支持收件文件，与运行日志分开，保留当日及此前 29 个日历日。通过联系邮箱发送的邮件由邮箱服务处理，可联系我们申请删除。
  • 网络基础设施：HTTPS、服务器、网关及云服务在传输和提供服务时需处理连接 IP 等网络信息。上述应用日志规则不表示整个网络链路不处理 IP；基础设施的安全记录由相应服务及配置管理。
  • 安全措施：识别请求通过 HTTPS 传输，服务器应用日志和反馈文件限制访问。第三方服务的保留安排见第 2 节；Apple iCloud 存储位置及保留取决于 Apple 服务和你的账户设置。

7. 管理与删除

你可在 App 中管理或删除本地账单，在系统 iCloud 存储设置管理云端副本，在「设置 → AI 数据与隐私」撤回当前设备的 AI 同意，或通过下方邮箱提出访问、更正或删除请求。删除 App 不一定删除 iCloud 副本，也不会自动取消订阅。为处理请求，我们可能需要与你核实必要的记录范围，请勿发送 Apple 密码或完整交易凭证。

Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker (iOS)

Effective: May 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Expense Tracker stores manual transactions on your device and, when enabled, in your private iCloud database. AI bookkeeping is optional. The entire selected image or submitted text is sent through our server to Volcengine Ark (Doubao models) and may contain personal data. Crop or remove unnecessary information before submitting.

1. Scope

This policy applies to bundle com.expense.ExpenseTracker. The explicit consent and withdrawal controls below apply to clients with the AI Data & Privacy page, including the initial release version 1.0.0. Older test clients without these controls must update to use AI. The backend deployed on November 17, 2026 rejects recognition requests lacking the supported consent version.

2. AI sharing: data, recipient and purpose

Only after you explicitly consent and initiate recognition via images, text, voice transcription, the share extension or a shortcut does the app send recognition content. The recipient is Volcengine Ark (Doubao models), provided by Beijing Volcano Engine Technology Co., Ltd., to extract bookkeeping fields such as amount, merchant, date, category and payment method.

  • Images: the complete selected or shared image/screenshot. Names, telephone numbers, addresses, account identifiers, orders and spending information visible in it may be transmitted. There is no automatic redaction.
  • Text: the complete text you submit, including voice transcription and any personal data it contains.
  • We do not automatically scan the full photo library or upload the entire existing ledger. Submit only content you are authorized to process and remove unrelated information first.

Data flows from the app through our HTTPS recognition backend to the Volcengine Ark Beijing-region API (ark.cn-beijing.volces.com). Our backend sends the selected image/text to the model. Quota identifiers and Apple signed transaction credentials are not forwarded as model prompt content.

The provider's processing and retention are governed by its applicable service terms, configuration and privacy policy. Our requests set store=false for model responses; this does not establish that the provider retains no security or operational records. We do not make unverified promises of provider zero retention or no model training.

3. Consent and choices

  • Before first AI use, the app displays the recipient, purpose, data categories, storage explanation and policy links. Upload requires selecting “Agree and Use AI.” An older AI feature toggle, image selection or microphone permission does not replace consent.
  • You may decline or close the disclosure. No new recognition content is sent; manual bookkeeping remains available.
  • Review or withdraw consent in Settings → AI Data & Privacy. Withdrawal stops future new uploads; it cannot automatically recall content already transmitted.
  • The consent version and timestamp are stored in the current device's shared app container for the app, share extension and shortcuts. They are not synchronized as ledger data. A shortcut without consent asks you to review and consent in the main app first.
  • Withdrawing consent does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it separately if no longer needed.

4. Other data and Apple services

  • Transactions: title, amount, category, date and notes are stored in local SwiftData and, if enabled, your private Apple CloudKit database. This does not automatically send your full ledger to our recognition backend. Content you select for recognition is handled as described above.
  • Quotas: our backend receives a random device identifier or a pseudonymous quota subject derived from verified transactions to count daily requests. These are pseudonymous identifiers, not a guarantee of irreversible anonymity.
  • Purchases: Apple StoreKit handles payment. The app may send Apple signed transaction credentials to our backend to verify subscription, product and quota. Credentials can include transaction identifiers, product, environment and validity information. We do not receive payment card details or send these credentials to the AI model.
  • Speech: Apple Speech performs transcription on-device when supported, and may use Apple's online service otherwise. Original recordings are not uploaded to our backend or Ark; transcribed text is sent for AI recognition with your consent.
  • Feedback: text you intentionally submit is used for support and may include contact or personal information you choose to provide. Avoid unrelated sensitive content.
  • Diagnostics: Apple may share crash reports when you allow developer analytics. The app does not use advertising IDFA or request contacts or location permissions.

Apple services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. System permissions

Image selection reads the image you choose. Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions allow recording and Apple transcription. You may decline these and continue manual bookkeeping. Manage private iCloud synchronization in system settings.

6. Storage, security and retention

  • Recognition content: our recognition service uses images, text and results while processing the request, without writing that content to business logs or a content database. Temporary multipart upload files are removed when the request ends. You may confirm results to save as transactions on your device.
  • Application logs: necessary operational metadata such as time, endpoint category, status and duration is retained. Recognition text, raw model output, transaction credentials and device identifiers are excluded. Logs rotate by UTC date and retain the current day and previous 29 calendar days with periodic cleanup.
  • Quota records: necessary pseudonymous subject, date and daily counters are retained for the current day and previous 29 calendar days. Cleanup runs after startup and hourly.
  • Feedback: intentional support feedback is kept in access-restricted support inbox files, separate from operational logs, for the current day and previous 29 calendar days. Email is handled by our email service; contact us to request deletion.
  • Infrastructure: HTTPS, hosting, gateways and cloud providers necessarily process IP addresses and other connection information. Application log exclusions do not mean that the entire network path processes no IP addresses; infrastructure security records depend on those services and configurations.
  • Security: recognition requests use HTTPS and server application log/feedback files have restricted access. Provider retention is described in section 2; iCloud location and retention depend on Apple services and your account settings.

7. Manage and delete

Manage or delete local transactions in the app, manage cloud copies in iCloud storage settings, and withdraw this device's consent in Settings → AI Data & Privacy. Contact us for access, correction or deletion requests. Deleting the app may not delete iCloud copies and does not cancel subscriptions. We may need to verify the relevant record scope; do not send Apple passwords or full transaction credentials.

8. Auto-renewing membership

  • Monthly Membership, product com.expense.ExpenseTracker.membership.monthly, auto-renews monthly at the price displayed by Apple.
  • The current daily AI quota is 3 for free users and 50 for members; unused requests do not roll over. AI still requires consent, permissions and network availability.
  • Apple may charge renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends unless cancelled at least 24 hours before that end. Manage subscriptions in system Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Request refunds through Apple, subject to applicable rules and Apple's policies.

9. Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. Minors should use it with guardian guidance and avoid submitting sensitive information. Contact us about children's data concerns. Material changes to sharing scope or recipients will be disclosed and consent requested again where needed.
